Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

ebookwala.github.io ("hum", "humara", "ye website") aapki privacy ka sammaan karta hai. Ye policy bataati hai ki hum kaunsi jaankari collect karte hain aur use kaise istemaal karte hain.

Jaankari jo hum collect karte hain

Hum directly aapse koi personal jaankari (jaise naam, email) nahi maangte, siwaye jab aap kisi ebook ko khareedte hain — us waqt payment process (Razorpay) aapki jaankari securely handle karta hai, hum us jaankari ko apne paas store nahi karte.

Cookies aur Advertising

Ye website Google AdSense ka istemaal karti hai ads dikhane ke liye. Google aur uske partners cookies ka istemaal kar sakte hain taaki aapko relevant ads dikhayi ja sakein, aapke is site aur doosri websites par visit ke aadhar par.

Aap Google Ads Settings (adssettings.google.com) par jaakar personalized advertising ko disable kar sakte hain. Third-party vendors, Google included, cookies ka istemaal karte hain users ko ads dikhane ke liye jo unki pichli visits par based hoti hain.

Third-party Links

Ye site payment ke liye Razorpay jaisi third-party services ka link use karti hai. In services ki apni alag privacy policies hoti hain, jinhe hum control nahi karte.

Contact

Is privacy policy se related kisi bhi sawal ke liye, aap humein website par diye gaye contact details se sampark kar sakte hain.
